\paragraph{Transfer helps under scarcity, with one exception.}
At the 12-scene budget, every pretrained ViT arm clears its random floor by a
wide margin: optical adds \HevDeltaFTenViTOptical, SAR
\HevDeltaFTenViTSar, and ImageNet \HevDeltaFTenViTImageNet{} F1 over
\HevDevFViTRandomTen. In the CNN track, SAR adds \HevDeltaFTenCNNSar{} and
ImageNet \HevDeltaFTenCNNImageNet{} over \HevDevFCNNRandomTen. The exception
is CNN-optical (BigEarthNet Sentinel-2), which only matches its floor at
10\% (both \HevDevFCNNOpticalTen) and falls below it at 25\% and 50\%. The
land-cover classification source appears to teach this backbone features
that vessel detection must first unlearn.

\paragraph{The SAR-versus-optical contrast is architecture-dependent.}
The CNN track orders SAR above optical at every budget, and the gap is large
under scarcity (\HevDevFCNNSarTen{} versus \HevDevFCNNOpticalTen{} at 12
scenes). The ViT track disagrees below half data: optical leads SAR at 10\%
(\HevDevFViTOpticalTen{} versus \HevDevFViTSarTen) and at 25\%, and SAR
recovers only from 56 scenes upward. A modality-matched encoder is therefore
not uniformly the best scarce-label choice; the architecture and pretraining
objective moderate the effect. Because the released checkpoints also differ
in objective and source volume, these contrasts describe available
checkpoints rather than a causal effect of modality alone.

\paragraph{Generic ImageNet wins at full data.}
With all 111 scenes, ImageNet initialization leads both tracks
(\HevDevFViTImageNetHundred{} and \HevDevFCNNImageNetHundred). Domain
pretraining matters most where labels are scarce; with enough target data,
generic visual structure plus full fine-tuning closes the domain gap.

\paragraph{Label efficiency.}
The best 12-scene transfer arms nearly match the 111-scene random floors:
ViT-optical reaches \HevDevFViTOpticalTen{} against the ViT floor's
\HevDevFViTRandomHundred{} at full data, and CNN-ImageNet reaches
\HevDevFCNNImageNetTen{} against \HevDevFCNNRandomHundred. Roughly ninety
percent of the labeling budget buys what a suitable public checkpoint
provides.
